# Financial Analysis – Anthem Biosciences (FY23 to Q2 FY27)

Prepare a comprehensive financial analysis of Anthem Biosciences using the available Annual Reports, quarterly results, investor presentations and earnings conference calls.

Cover FY23, FY24, FY25, FY26 and FY27 up to Q2 (H1 FY27 wherever applicable).

Organize the analysis into the following sections:

### 1. Profit & Loss Analysis
Prepare a table containing:
- Revenue from Operations
- EBITDA
- EBITDA Margin (%)
- Incremental Revenue
- Incremental EBITDA
- Incremental EBITDA Margin (%)
- Interest Expense
- Depreciation
- Profit Before Tax (PBT)
- Profit After Tax (PAT)
- PAT Margin (%)
- NOPAT
- NOPAT Margin (%)

Calculate incremental EBITDA margin as Incremental EBITDA / Incremental Revenue.

Calculate NOPAT as EBIT × (1 – effective tax rate), using a consistent methodology. Clearly disclose the tax rate used.

### 2. Cash Flow Analysis
Prepare a table containing:
- Cash Flow from Operations (CFO)
- Capital Expenditure
- Free Cash Flow (FCF)
- Net Cash Flow
- EBITDA
- CFO / EBITDA (%)

Explain significant differences between EBITDA and operating cash flow.

### 3. Balance Sheet Analysis
Prepare a table containing:
- Gross Debt
- Cash and Cash Equivalents
- Net Debt / Net Cash
- Total Equity
- Gross Fixed Assets
- Net Fixed Assets
- Capital Work in Progress (CWIP)
- Total Assets
- Capital Employed

### 4. Working Capital Analysis
Capture the historical trend in:
- Trade Receivables
- Inventory
- Trade Payables
- Net Working Capital
- Receivable Days
- Inventory Days
- Payable Days
- Cash Conversion Cycle

Explain significant changes and their impact on cash generation.

### 5. Return Ratios – DuPont Analysis
Perform a three-factor DuPont analysis of ROE:
- Net Profit Margin
- Asset Turnover
- Equity Multiplier
- ROE

Also calculate ROCE and explain its movement using:
- EBIT Margin
- Capital Employed Turnover

Clearly state the methodology and denominator used for all ratios.

### 6. Incremental Capital Productivity
This is particularly important for our J-Curve and Non-Linear Capital study.

Calculate:
- Incremental Revenue
- Incremental EBITDA
- Incremental EBITDA Margin
- Incremental EBIT
- Incremental NOPAT
- Incremental Capital Employed
- Incremental ROCE (Incremental NOPAT / Incremental Capital Employed)
- Incremental Asset Turnover
- Capex / Incremental Revenue
- CFO / EBITDA

Explain whether incremental earnings are growing faster or slower than incremental capital employed.

### 7. Capex and Capital Deployment
Prepare a separate table showing:
- Annual Capital Expenditure
- Capitalization of Fixed Assets
- CWIP
- Depreciation
- Gross Block
- Net Block

Identify major capacity expansions and explain how capital deployment relates to revenue and earnings growth.

### 8. Key Observations
Summarize:
- Revenue growth versus EBITDA growth.
- Historical incremental EBITDA margins.
- Operating leverage trends.
- Cash flow conversion.
- Working capital efficiency.
- ROE and ROCE trends.
- Incremental capital productivity.
- Whether Anthem is showing characteristics of a J-Curve.

### Important Instructions
1. Use company-reported figures wherever available. Do not invent missing numbers.
2. Clearly distinguish reported figures from calculated figures.
3. Show all financial values in ₹ crore.
4. For FY27 Q2, provide both Q2 standalone and H1 FY27 cumulative figures wherever available. Do not compare quarterly figures directly with full-year figures.
5. For incremental calculations, use comparable periods. Clearly specify the comparison base.
6. Use average opening and closing balances for ROE, ROCE, asset turnover and working capital ratios wherever possible.
7. For consolidated analysis, consistently use consolidated financial statements.
8. Explain any changes in accounting treatment, exceptional items or classification that affect comparability.
9. Cite the relevant Annual Report, quarterly result or investor presentation and page number for every major reported figure.
10. If information is unavailable, explicitly mark it as 'Not Disclosed'.

The objective is to create a reliable historical financial database for subsequent J-Curve, operating leverage and Non-Linear Capital analysis. Do not make stock valuation recommendations.
