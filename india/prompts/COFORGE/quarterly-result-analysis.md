# COFORGE — QUARTERLY RESULT REVIEW

## OBJECTIVE

Review ONLY the latest reported quarter of Coforge Limited and the corresponding earnings conference call / investor presentation.

The purpose is to determine:

"What actually changed this quarter in Coforge's financial performance, operating performance, management confidence, expectations and earnings trajectory?"

This is a quarterly change-detection exercise.

Do NOT explain the entire business model.

Do NOT perform valuation.

Do NOT perform competitive analysis.

Do NOT provide an investment recommendation.

Do NOT repeat previously known information merely because management repeated it.

Always prioritize the latest quarter.

Use earlier disclosures only when necessary to establish what management had previously expected or guided.

If evidence is insufficient, write:

"NOT SUFFICIENTLY DISCLOSED."

# IMPORTANT SOURCE DISCIPLINE

Always distinguish:

FACT

from

MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY

from

ANALYST REACTION

from

INTERPRETATION.

Do not convert management claims into facts.

Do not convert analyst questions into analyst opinions unless the wording explicitly indicates an opinion.

Do not convert industry opportunity into Coforge-specific demand.

Do not convert pipeline into order intake.

Do not convert order intake into revenue.

Do not convert order book into immediate revenue.

Do not convert AI pilots into AI revenue.

Do not convert AI productivity claims into margin expansion unless supported by evidence.

Do not convert acquisition revenue into organic growth.

Do not convert management aspiration into guidance.

Do not use subsequent developments.

Keep the analysis concise.

The objective is a repeatable quarterly review that makes it easy to identify:

1. What happened?
2. What was genuinely new?
3. What changed versus previous expectations?
4. What did management become more or less confident about?
5. Which financial assumptions relevant to future earnings changed?
