# QUESTIONS TO ANSWER

## 1. HOW DOES MANAGEMENT THINK?

Identify the recurring principles that appear to guide management's decisions.

Focus on:

• How they think about growth

• How they think about clients

• How they think about technology and AI

• How they think about talent

• How they think about acquisitions

• How they think about margins

• How they think about execution

Do not simply repeat management's stated strategy.

Look for patterns across multiple periods.

------------------------------------------------

EVIDENCE OF PROGRESS

## 3. HOW HAS THEIR STRATEGY EVOLVED?

Identify the most important changes in management's strategic thinking.

Use:

EARLIER APPROACH
→
WHAT CHANGED
→
CURRENT APPROACH

## 4. ARE THEY REACTIVE OR PROACTIVE?

Assess whether management generally:

• Anticipates industry changes

• Responds after changes occur

• Proactively reallocates resources

• Proactively changes the business model

Support the assessment with specific evidence.

Do not infer "proactive" merely because management uses proactive language.

------------------------------------------------

## 5. WHAT ARE THEY OPTIMIZING FOR?

Assess the relative importance management appears to place on:

• Revenue growth

• Earnings growth

• Margin

• ROCE / capital efficiency

• Cash generation

• Resilience

• Market share

• Client relationships

• Optionality

Identify the trade-offs management appears willing to make.

------------------------------------------------

## 6. WHAT DO THEY WORRY ABOUT?

Identify recurring concerns, risks or problems management discusses.

Pay particular attention to:

• What they repeatedly emphasize

• What they monitor closely

• What they are trying to prevent

• What they appear unwilling to compromise on

------------------------------------------------

## 7. WHAT HAVE THEY STOPPED TALKING ABOUT?

Compare recent management commentary with earlier periods.

Identify important topics, initiatives or claims that management:

• Talks about less

• Has stopped discussing

• Has changed its language around

• Appears to have deprioritized

Do not assume that silence means abandonment.

State when the evidence is insufficient.

------------------------------------------------

Track changes in:

• Strength of commitments

• Timing expectations

• Willingness to make specific targets

• Changes in previously stated expectations

Identify whether management's confidence appears:

INCREASING
STABLE
DECREASING
or
UNCLEAR

Explain why.
