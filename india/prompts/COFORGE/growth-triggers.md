# OBJECTIVE

Identify the growth triggers most likely to ACCELERATE Coforge Limited's consolidated earnings over the next 6–12 months.

This is NOT a business model exercise.

This is NOT a unit economics exercise.

This is NOT a valuation exercise.

This is NOT a competitive analysis.

Do NOT identify every growth opportunity.

Instead, identify only the developments with the highest probability of materially changing the RATE OF CHANGE of Revenue, EBITDA, EBIT and PAT over the next 2–4 quarters.

The central question is:

"What can cause Coforge's earnings growth to accelerate from its current trajectory?"

Give significantly greater weight to:

• Earnings acceleration

• Incremental revenue

• Incremental margins

• Large-deal conversion

• Order-book conversion

• Organic growth acceleration

• Account expansion

• Acquisition contribution / integration

• Utilization and productivity

• Service / delivery mix

• AI-led revenue or productivity

• Operating leverage

Give substantially less weight to opportunities whose financial impact is primarily beyond the next 12 months.

Always prioritize the latest disclosures.

Use older disclosures mainly to determine whether management expectations have:

• Strengthened

• Weakened

• Accelerated

• Been delayed

• Become more specific

• Become less specific

Never assume financial impact that is not supported by the available documents.

Whenever evidence is insufficient, explicitly state:

"EVIDENCE INSUFFICIENT."

------------------------------------------------

# OUTPUT FORMAT — STRICT

For every section produce:

A. FACTS

B. MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY

C. BRIEF INTERPRETATION
(maximum 3 bullet points)

Do not mix facts and interpretation.

Spend the majority of the response on FACTS and MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY.

Do not create unsupported quantitative estimates.
