# Sansera: Value Chain, Unit Economics & Capability Architecture

## Objective

Develop a first-principles understanding of **how Sansera converts engineering and manufacturing capability into economic value**.

The objective is to understand the business at the level of:

**Raw material → engineering → forging → machining → finishing/assembly → qualification → customer program → component revenue**

Do not perform a full financial analysis, valuation analysis, or growth forecast.

Do not analyse stock price, valuation multiples, ROCE, free cash flow, or detailed margin trends. Those will be addressed separately.

Instead, explain the **economic mechanics of the business** that will later allow us to understand unit economics, growth drivers and financial performance.

Use **only the uploaded documents**.

Clearly distinguish: between "Documented Facts" and "Interpretation". Keep "Interpretation" to 2 or 3 bullet points only.

Do not convert management aspirations into facts.
