# Objective: Deepen Understanding of Garware Hi-Tech Films' Core Products, Market Position, Manufacturing Capacity and Distribution Model

Having established a broad understanding of Garware Hi-Tech Films' business model, investigate the company's key product categories, their commercial importance, market position, manufacturing capacity and routes to market.

The objective is to understand which products are central to the company's business, how its manufacturing infrastructure supports these products, how much additional capacity is being created and how its distribution network converts manufactured films into commercial revenue.

Maintain a first-principles approach and institutional-quality research depth.

## 1. Identify the Company's Most Important Products

I suspect that Solar Control Films (SCF) and Paint Protection Films (PPF) are among the most important products in Garware's portfolio. Verify this using the available company disclosures rather than accepting this assumption.

- Identify the major product categories contributing to the company's revenue.
- What is the individual revenue contribution of Solar Control Films, Paint Protection Films and other important product categories, wherever disclosed?
- What percentage of total revenue does each product category contribute?
- How has the contribution of these products evolved over the last 3–5 years?
- Is the contribution of SCF and PPF increasing relative to the company's other products?
- How important are these products within the Consumer Product Division (CPD)?
- What is the relative importance of CPD and IPD to the overall business?
- Are there other products within IPD or CPD that are commercially significant and deserve similar attention?
- Distinguish between management's strategic emphasis on a product and its actual contribution to reported revenue.
- Where product-level revenue is not disclosed, identify the closest available information without manufacturing estimates.

Present a table showing product category, end-use application, revenue contribution, historical trend and source of information.

Clearly distinguish between:
- Disclosed product-level revenue.
- Disclosed divisional revenue.
- Management commentary about product importance.
- Analytical interpretation.

**B. Paint Protection Films**

- Does it disclose market share in important international markets?
- Are there separate market share disclosures for premium PPF products or other product categories?
- If historical figures are available, explain the trend.

**C. Other Important Products**

- Does the company disclose market share for any important IPD products, particularly shrink films or other specialty industrial films?
- Identify any available market share information for these products.

For all market share information:

1. Clearly identify the source and reporting period.
2. Distinguish between company-reported figures, management estimates and third-party industry estimates.
3. Identify the geographical market and product category to which each figure relates.
4. Do not combine market share figures based on different market definitions.
5. Do not estimate market share from revenue or capacity unless the necessary market-size information is explicitly available.
6. If the company does not disclose market share, explicitly state: "Not established by the available sources."

This section is about extracting disclosed market position, not conducting an independent competitor analysis.

## 3. Manufacturing Capacity, Utilisation and Expansion Plans

Develop a detailed understanding of Garware's manufacturing infrastructure, with particular emphasis on SCF and PPF.

**A. Existing Manufacturing Capacity**

Identify the latest available installed manufacturing capacity for each important product category.

At a minimum, investigate:

- Polyester chips.
- Base BOPET/industrial films.
- Solar Control Films.
- Paint Protection Films.
- Thermal Lamination Films.
- Other important specialty film categories wherever capacity is separately disclosed.

For each product category, identify:

- Installed annual capacity.
- Unit of measurement.
- Manufacturing location.
- Date on which the capacity became operational.
- Whether the capacity is dedicated to that product or shared with other products.
- Actual production, wherever disclosed.
- Current or latest available capacity utilisation.
- Historical capacity utilisation trend, wherever available.

Clearly distinguish between installed capacity, effective capacity, production and sales volumes.

If capacity utilisation is not disclosed at the individual product level, do not calculate or assume it without supporting information.

**B. Capacity Expansion and Capital Expenditure**

Identify all significant ongoing, recently completed and announced manufacturing expansion projects.

Pay particular attention to:

1. Solar Control Film capacity expansion.
2. Paint Protection Film capacity expansion.
3. TPU extrusion/backward integration project.
4. Any other relevant specialty-film manufacturing expansion.

For each project, explain:

- Product category and purpose of the project.
- Announced capital expenditure.
- Additional manufacturing capacity being created.
- Manufacturing location.
- Announcement date and subsequent progress updates.
- Whether the project is planned, under construction, installed, commissioned or commercially operational.
- Expected commissioning date.
- Expected commercial production start date, if different.
- Expected time required for capacity ramp-up, wherever disclosed.
- Whether the project is progressing according to the original timeline.
- Any changes in project cost, capacity or commissioning schedule.

**C. Funding of Capital Expenditure**

- How is the company funding its ongoing and planned capital expenditure?
- Is the funding expected to come from internal accruals, existing cash reserves, borrowings or other sources?
- Has management explicitly indicated the funding arrangement?
- Does the company expect to incur additional debt?
- Are there any disclosed financing arrangements or commitments related to these projects?

Clearly distinguish between announced funding plans and actual funding already secured or deployed.

**D. Capacity Expansion and the Operating Model**

- How will additional SCF and PPF capacity fit into the existing manufacturing structure?
- Does the company have the flexibility to shift manufacturing capacity between different product grades or categories?
- Are the new capacities intended primarily for domestic demand, exports or both?
- Does the company disclose the expected product mix from the expanded facilities?
- Are there any dependencies on raw material availability, technology, customer approvals or distribution infrastructure before the new capacity can reach commercial production?

Avoid detailed project valuation or return-on-capital calculations. The objective is to understand the manufacturing footprint and operational readiness.

Present a consolidated capacity and capex tracker in table format.

## 5. Garware Application Studios (GAS) and Garware Home Solutions (GHS)

Investigate the role of GAS and GHS in Garware's distribution and customer acquisition model.

- Are there disclosed plans for further expansion?
- What is the geographical distribution of these studios?
- Are the studios concentrated in major cities or expanding into smaller cities?
- How many studios are in India and how many are overseas, wherever disclosed?

**B. Garware Home Solutions (GHS)**

**C. Financial and Commercial Model of GAS and GHS**

## Important Research Instructions

- Prioritize the latest company disclosures. Older disclosures should primarily be used to establish historical trends.
- Clearly identify the reporting period, units and source for every important number.
- Separate FACTS, MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY, INTERPRETATION and UNANSWERED QUESTIONS.
- Attribute all management estimates, targets and expectations explicitly to management.
- Do not treat announced capacity as commissioned capacity.
- Do not treat commissioned capacity as commercially ramped-up capacity.
- Do not assume that installed capacity is fully utilised.
- Do not infer product-wise revenue or market share when the company has not disclosed it.
- Do not confuse studio count with operational or revenue-generating studio count.
- Do not assume that all GAS and GHS locations follow the same ownership or financial model.
- Do not treat the number of studios as proof of commercial success.
- Do not invent market share, capacity utilisation, product revenue, studio economics or capex funding arrangements.
- Wherever information is insufficient, explicitly state: "Not established by the available sources."
- Wherever possible, provide the source document and page number supporting important factual statements.

**Scope restriction:** This exercise is focused on product importance, disclosed market position, manufacturing capacity, expansion projects, the first-principles value chain and distribution channels. Do not undertake detailed unit economics, product-wise profitability analysis, competitive benchmarking, valuation, DuPont analysis or investment recommendations. These will be examined separately.
