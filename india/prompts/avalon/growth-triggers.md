OBJECTIVE

Identify the most important GROWTH TRIGGERS for Avalon Technologies Limited over the next 6–12 months.

I am NOT simply looking for factors that can grow revenue.

I am particularly interested in triggers capable of causing a SIGNIFICANT POSITIVE CHANGE IN THE RATE OF EARNINGS GROWTH.

The central question is:

"What developments could cause Avalon's EBITDA and PAT growth to accelerate materially beyond the current expected trajectory over the next 2–4 quarters?"

Think in terms of:

NORMAL GROWTH DRIVER
vs.
EARNINGS ACCELERATOR
vs.
POTENTIAL STEP-CHANGE TRIGGER.

A growth trigger becomes especially important when it can produce one or more of:

• materially higher revenue growth
• higher incremental EBITDA margins
• operating leverage
• removal of an existing loss/drag
• major new program revenue
• significantly higher customer wallet share
• better business mix
• materially higher ROCE
• significantly stronger cash generation
• repeated upward revisions to management guidance

Use ONLY the uploaded Avalon documents.

Prioritize the MOST RECENT information.

Use older documents to determine how expectations, timelines and management confidence have changed.

Do NOT discuss valuation, PE multiples or stock price.
