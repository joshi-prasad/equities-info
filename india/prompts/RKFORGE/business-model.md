Analyse RKFORGE's business model from first principles.

The objective is to understand WHAT THE COMPANY ACTUALLY DOES and HOW IT CREATES VALUE FOR ITS CUSTOMERS.

Do not turn this into a financial analysis, growth-trigger analysis, management philosophy analysis, detailed unit-economics analysis, or bull/base/bear analysis. Those topics will be analysed separately.

The goal is to build a clear mental model of the business.

========================================
1. WHAT DOES RKFORGE ACTUALLY MANUFACTURE?
========================================

Explain in simple but technically accurate terms what RKFORGE actually makes.

Identify:

• the major categories of products/components it manufactures
• what these components physically are
• where they are used
• which end-products/vehicles/equipment they ultimately become part of
• the major manufacturing processes used to produce them
• which products/processes are core to the existing business
• which products/processes are relatively newer

Do not simply reproduce the company's product list.

Group the products into logical categories and explain what each category means in practical terms.

For each major product category, answer:

"What is the component?"

"What does it do in the customer's final product?"

"Why is this component technically important?"

Avoid unnecessary technical detail unless it helps explain the business.

========================================

• development and qualification requirements
• cost competitiveness

Identify the specific customer problems that the documents support.

Explain WHY these problems matter to an OEM or Tier-1 customer.
