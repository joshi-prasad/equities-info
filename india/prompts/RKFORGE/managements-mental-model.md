For every conclusion, distinguish between:

FACT
What management explicitly said or did.

MANAGEMENT MENTAL MODEL
What can reasonably be inferred from repeated statements, decisions and changes in behaviour.

Do not infer management's thinking from a single statement.

Give greater weight to repeated patterns across recent quarters, and prioritize the most recent commentary when management's thinking appears to have changed.

Keep the interpretation concise and evidence-based.

1. How does management think?

2. What problems are they trying to solve?

3. How has their strategy evolved?

4. Are they reactive or proactive?

5. Do they optimize primarily for growth, ROCE, resilience, or optionality?

6. Which trade-offs are they consciously making?

7. What does management believe is the biggest constraint to growth?

8. What does management believe will drive the next phase of earnings growth?

9. What does management appear to worry about?

10. What has management stopped talking about?

11. How has management's confidence evolved?
