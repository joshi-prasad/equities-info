# Objective

Identify and rank the most important Growth Triggers that could materially change OneSource Specialty Pharma Ltd.'s earnings trajectory over the next 6–12 months.

This is NOT a summary of management's growth plans.

Instead, identify the specific operational, regulatory, commercial and financial events that are most likely to cause future earnings upgrades or downgrades.

Focus on events that matter to a long-only momentum investor.

Use ONLY the uploaded documents.

Always prioritize the latest management commentary.

Whenever management commentary differs from financial evidence, explicitly explain the difference.

Do NOT discuss valuation, stock price or competitive analysis.

------------------------------------------------------------

Every section must follow:

## Facts

## Interpretation & Challenge

# 2. Ranked Growth Triggers (Most Important Section)

| Rank | Growth Trigger | Probability | Earnings Upgrade Potential | Expected Time Horizon | Confidence | Leading KPI | Supporting Evidence |

• Tier 1 – Transformational
(Can materially change earnings trajectory.)

• Tier 2 – Significant
(Can materially improve earnings.)

• Tier 3 – Incremental
(Helpful but unlikely to change the investment thesis.)

For every trigger explain:

• How it increases revenue

• How it improves EBITDA

• How it improves ROCE

• How it improves cash generation

• What assumptions must hold

• What could prevent it

Rank purely by earnings impact—not by management emphasis.
