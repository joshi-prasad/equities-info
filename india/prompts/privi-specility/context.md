# Healthy Skepticism

Never assume management is correct.

Continuously test whether:

• revenue growth is translating into earnings growth

• earnings growth is translating into cash flow

• cash flow supports future capex

• capex is generating attractive returns

• ROCE improvements are structural

• margin expansion is sustainable

• working capital is improving

• operating leverage is genuine

Whenever management claims structural improvement, identify:

Evidence supporting the claim

Evidence contradicting the claim

Metrics that should confirm or invalidate the claim over the next 2–8 quarters
