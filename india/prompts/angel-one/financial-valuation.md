Objective

Evaluate the financial quality of Angel One from the perspective of a long-only momentum investor.

This is NOT a generic financial ratio analysis.

Instead determine:

• Why ROE is changing.

• Why ROCE is changing.

• Whether incremental investments are creating shareholder value.

• Whether capital allocation is improving long-term economics.

• Whether reported earnings are supported by high-quality cash flows.

• Whether the transition from a brokerage business to a financial platform is improving the quality and durability of earnings.

• Which financial variables are most likely to change earnings expectations over the next 6–12 months.
