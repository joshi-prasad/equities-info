Objective

Identify the variables most likely to change Angel One's earnings expectations over the next 6–12 months.

This is NOT a generic growth analysis.

Instead, identify:

• What is actually driving earnings today.

• What management believes will drive earnings tomorrow.

• Which narratives are already supported by financial evidence.

• Which narratives remain aspirational.

• Which developments could positively or negatively change earnings expectations over the next 2–8 quarters.

Do NOT discuss valuation, intrinsic value or stock price.

Do NOT perform financial ratio analysis (ROE, ROCE, DuPont, etc.). That will be covered separately.

Use ONLY the uploaded documents:

• FY26 Annual Report
• FY25 Q4 – FY26 Q4 Investor Presentations
• FY25 Q4 – FY26 Q4 Earnings Call Transcripts
• CARE Ratings Report
• Monthly Business Updates
• Financial Statements (only where required to validate management commentary)

Always prioritize the most recent documents whenever information has evolved.

Maintain an opinionated but moderately skeptical mindset.

Whenever management commentary differs from financial evidence, explicitly discuss the difference.

Always distinguish between:

• Fact
• Management Commentary
• Interpretation
• Hypothesis
