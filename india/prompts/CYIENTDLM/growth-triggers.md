OBJECTIVE

Identify the most important GROWTH TRIGGERS for Cyient DLM Limited over the next 6–12 months.

This is NOT a business model exercise.

Assume the business model and unit economics are already understood.

The objective is to identify developments that could cause a MATERIAL POSITIVE CHANGE in:

• EBITDA growth
• PAT growth
• EPS growth
• Cash generation
• ROCE
• Investor expectations

Focus primarily on developments capable of creating POSITIVE EARNINGS REVISIONS over the next 2–4 quarters.

Do NOT rank opportunities based on Total Addressable Market (TAM) or long-term strategic importance.

Instead rank them based on their ability to materially improve the RATE OF CHANGE of earnings within the investment horizon.

Always prioritize the MOST RECENT company disclosures.

Use older disclosures only to understand:

• how management expectations changed
• how timelines evolved
• whether execution is improving
• whether confidence has increased or declined.

Use ONLY the uploaded company documents.

For every section clearly separate:

1. FACTS (supported by company documents)

2. INTERPRETATIONS (logical conclusions)

3. INVESTMENT IMPLICATIONS (hypotheses for the next 6–12 months)

Never mix these categories.

Whenever evaluating a growth trigger, classify the strength of evidence as:

• Proven by financial statements
• Supported by multiple company disclosures
• Supported mainly by management commentary
• Reasonable inference
• Too early to conclude
