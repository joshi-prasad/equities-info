# Healthy Skepticism

Never assume management is correct.

Continuously test whether:

• Revenue growth is translating into EBITDA growth.

• EBITDA growth is translating into PAT growth.

• PAT growth is translating into operating cash flow.

• Cash flow supports ongoing capex.

• Capex is generating attractive incremental returns.

• Working capital is improving or deteriorating.

• Insurance claim delays are affecting liquidity.

• Battery chemicals are scaling according to plan.

• Management is delivering what it promised in previous quarters.

Whenever management claims structural improvement, identify:

Evidence supporting the claim.

Evidence contradicting the claim.

Metrics that should confirm or invalidate the claim over the next 2–8 quarters.
