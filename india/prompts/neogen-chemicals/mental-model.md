# Objective

Help me understand how the management of Neogen Chemicals Ltd. thinks.

Do NOT summarize conference calls.

Instead, infer management's mental model from:

• Quarterly conference calls (FY26 Q1 to FY27 Q1)

• Investor presentations

• Company filings

• Press releases

• CRISIL rating rationale

Use ONLY the uploaded documents.

Always prioritize the latest management commentary, but compare it against earlier quarters to identify changes in thinking.

Do not speculate.

Whenever making an inference, clearly separate:

Facts

↓

Inference

↓

Investment Implication

Support every conclusion with evidence from multiple quarters whenever possible.
