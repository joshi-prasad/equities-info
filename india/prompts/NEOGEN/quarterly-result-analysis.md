# SPECIAL NEOGEN DEFINITIONS / DISCIPLINE

For this analysis, maintain the following distinctions:

### 1. Organolithium is NOT a battery chemical.

Organolithium belongs to Neogen's legacy Organic Chemicals business and should be analysed separately.

Do not combine Organolithium growth with Neogen Ionics / battery revenue.

### 2. Battery chemicals consist primarily of:

- Electrolytes
- Lithium electrolyte salts / LiPF6

Analyse these separately wherever management provides sufficient information.

### 3. Domestic and export battery businesses are different.

Where disclosed, distinguish:

**Domestic electrolytes**

from

**Export lithium salts.**

Do not assume that weakness in domestic electrolyte demand automatically means weakness in export salt demand.

### 4. Commercialization stages must remain separate.

Do not equate:

**Customer interest → Trial → Audit → PPAP → LTA → Purchase Order → Commercial Shipment → Repeat Shipment → Sustained Utilisation**

These are separate milestones.

### 5. Lithium prices

Where management uses formula-linked pricing, distinguish between:

- Revenue movement caused by lithium/raw-material prices
- Actual volume growth
- Conversion margin
- EBITDA growth

Do not automatically interpret higher/lower revenue caused by lithium prices as underlying business growth/decline.

### 6. Battery economics

Focus on:

**Volume × Conversion Margin × Utilisation**

rather than simply lithium price movements.

Do not invent EBITDA/tonne, breakeven utilization or ROCE calculations unless the available data supports them.

### 7. Balance sheet

Pay particular attention to:

- Debt
- Interest expense
- Working capital
- Capex
- Insurance proceeds
- Cash flow
- Deleveraging

### 8. Legacy business

- Organic Chemicals

- CSM / advanced intermediates
- Organolithium
- Margins
- Capacity utilisation

### 9. Operating leverage
