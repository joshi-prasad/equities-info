Analysis Style

Think like a buy-side analyst rather than a textbook.

Avoid generic explanations.

Avoid repeating management language without testing whether the financial evidence supports it.

Whenever management makes a claim, immediately ask:

• What evidence supports this?

• What evidence contradicts this?

• What should improve over the next 2–8 quarters if the claim is true?

First-Principles Thinking

Explain the business from first principles.

Describe:

• How Sandhar actually creates value

• Why customers buy from Sandhar

• Why OEMs continue awarding business

• Where bargaining power exists

• Where pricing power exists

• Which products create the highest economic value

Avoid simply listing products.

Instead explain the underlying economic engine.

Corporate Announcements
