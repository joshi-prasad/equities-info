# Cummins India Ltd

*Date:* 7 August 2026

## FY27Q1 Result

### SECTION 1 — OVERALL RESULT SCORE

| Item | Assessment | Evidence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Revenue Growth** | **Strong** | Standalone sales grew by **18% YoY** to ₹3,375 Cr and **14% QoQ**. Standalone domestic sales led the performance, expanding by **22% YoY** to ₹2,854 Cr. |
| **Margin Performance** | **Weak** | Standalone PBT margin (before exceptional items) contracted to **21.4%**, which is lower by 0.7% YoY and lower by 12% QoQ (down from 25.4% in Q1 FY26 and 27.7% in Q4 FY26). Margins were severely compressed by unprecedented raw material cost inflation (steel, pig iron, copper, aluminum) and rising freight costs. |
| **Profit Growth** | **Weak** | Standalone PBT (before exceptional items) fell **0.7% YoY** to ₹721 Cr. Standalone PAT declined **7.85% YoY** to ₹543.01 Cr (down 16.48% QoQ). Consolidated PAT was virtually flat at **+0.9% YoY** (₹609.30 Cr). |
| **Cash Flow / Working Capital** | **NOT SUFFICIENTLY DISCLOSED** | No quarterly cash flow statement or overall working capital figures were published in the Q1 financial results. |
| **Balance Sheet** | **NOT SUFFICIENTLY DISCLOSED** | No quarterly balance sheet was published in the Q1 financial results. (The press release qualitatively notes a "healthy balance sheet and strong liquidity position", but no reported numbers were provided). |
| **Overall Quarter** | **Average** | The quarter showcased robust volume and top-line sales growth driven by domestic demand, but profitability was severely neutralized by sudden commodity inflation and freight headwinds. |

### SECTION 3 — IMPORTANT MANAGEMENT COMMENTS

#### A. Bullish Comments
| Comment | Why it Matters |
| :--- | :--- |
| *"Power generation domestic sales are at 1,424 crores that's a 35% increase over last year"*. | Demonstrates explosive volume growth in the core domestic power generation business, driven by heavy data center project execution. |
| *"We are not only generating inquiries for this year but we are now talking about the next year and the year after that"* on data centers. | Establishes a long-term, multi-year pipeline for high-capacity power solutions, improving revenue visibility beyond the current fiscal year. |
| *"all the data center customers are wanting us to prep our deliveries to them"*. | Confirms extreme demand intensity and customer urgency, indicating that data center developers are not experiencing project delays or slowdowns. |
| *"distribution business absolutely has the potential to grow at 20% growth over this year and the next few years as well"*. | Reassures investors that the 14% growth recorded this quarter is temporary, and the high-margin aftermarket/services business has significant runway. |

#### B. Cautious / Bearish Comments
| Comment | Why it Matters |
| :--- | :--- |
| *"...commodity related cost inflation also has been impacting... steel, pig [iron] and aluminium, copper significant increases... freight costs have been increasing"*. | Identifies the main drivers of the severe margin pinch, signaling that high commodity and shipping costs will continue to squeeze profitability. |
| *"Price realization takes at least a quarter... will we be able to recover all commodity increases through this price rise [in Q2]? No"*. | Warns that the July 2026 price hike will have a lag in realization due to order backlogs, and will not fully offset the raw material cost surge. |
| *"West Asia crisis is actually leading to some of our exports not going to the Middle East as anticipated... these orders do not stay that long [and get fulfilled locally]"*. | Points to immediate geopolitical revenue loss in low horsepower exports that cannot simply be deferred and recovered in subsequent quarters. |
| *"Construction largely is flattish... there are no new road construction highway national highway construction tenders coming in"*. | Identifies domestic infrastructure policy slowdowns as a temporary headwind in the domestic industrial segment. |
