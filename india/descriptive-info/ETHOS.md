# Business Model

### **1. Business Model of Ethos Limited**

Ethos Limited is India’s largest and most prominent luxury and premium watch retailer. The company operates a specialized **omnichannel luxury retail model** that blends an expansive physical store network with digital platforms. Across physical retail, it operates multiple store formats, including multi-brand Ethos Watch Boutiques, flagship Ethos Summit stores, Haute Horology lounges, and exclusive mono-brand boutiques for globally renowned maisons. The company has established partnerships with over 80 leading international watchmakers, maintaining exclusive Indian retail agreements with 64 brands. Its product offering covers four distinct price tiers: Premium, Bridge to Luxury, Luxury, and High Luxury. The company's average selling price (ASP) has consistently expanded, reaching **₹2.07 lakh** in FY26 and **₹2.26 lakh** in Q1 FY27.

Because high-end timepieces are manufactured abroad, Ethos sources virtually 100% of its watch inventory internationally via direct imports, primarily from Switzerland. In addition to new luxury timepieces, Ethos operates a **Certified Pre-Owned (CPO)** vertical branded as *Second Movement*, providing authentication, refurbishment, and resale of pre-owned luxury watches. The company also operates an integrated after-sales and service division called *Ethos Watch Care*, which repairs and services timepieces across more than 70 luxury brands using certified watchmakers.

To monetize its high-net-worth individual (HNI) clientele, Ethos has diversified into adjacent luxury lifestyle categories—such as exclusive distribution of **Messika** (fine jewellery) and **RIMOWA** (luxury luggage)—housed under its subsidiary Ethos Lifestyle Private Limited. Online discovery and client advisory are driven by in-house digital assets, including the *Watch Guide* editorial publication, the *Ethos App*, and an in-house media production unit named *Ethos Studio*. Revenue is generated primarily from the markup and retail sale of goods, supplemented by maintenance and repair service income. In essence, Ethos acts as the definitive organized gateway, curator, and custodian for global luxury brands entering the Indian consumer market.

### **2. Business Verticals, P&L Statement, and Sales Contribution**

#### **Business Verticals Overview**
Ethos operates across three principal verticals:
1. **Luxury Retail (Watches & Lifestyle)**: Retail trading of new luxury watches and adjacent lifestyle merchandise (Messika jewellery and Rimowa luggage).
2. **Certified Pre-Owned (CPO)**: Authentication, buying, and selling of pre-owned luxury watches under *Second Movement*.
3. **Service Vertical**: After-sales repair, maintenance, and servicing of fine mechanical timepieces via *Ethos Watch Care* and *Micron Watch Services*.

#### **P&L Segment Reporting Status**
Under **Ind AS 108**, Ethos reports as a **single operating segment**: *"retail trading of premium and luxury watches, accessories and other luxury items and including related after sale services"*. Consequently, discrete, full P&L statements for individual verticals are not reported separately in the audited financial statements. In statutory BRSR filings, retail trading of watches and watch accessories accounts for **100%** of turnover.

However, company-wide consolidated financials and standalone subsidiary performance provide the overall financial profile and confirm that **Luxury Watch Retail is overwhelmingly the most important vertical**, driving over **98%** of total revenue.

### **6. Vertical Integration Story**

Ethos has orchestrated a comprehensive vertical integration and luxury lifecycle strategy across both upstream brand positioning and downstream customer touchpoints:

```
[Upstream Lineage & IP]          [Core Omnichannel Retail]            [Downstream Lifecycle]
  KDDL Limited (Promoter)           103 Boutiques (34 Cities)           Ethos Watch Care & Micron
  (Dial & Component Maker)        (Summit, Mono-brand, Haute Horology)   (Service & Maintenance)
            │                                  │                                  │
  Silvercity Brands AG /                       │                        Second Movement
  Favre Leuba (Brand Ownership)                │                      (Certified Pre-Owned)
            │                                  │                                  │
  Haute-rive Watches SA              Ethos App & Digital Media               Ethos Lifestyle
  (Independent Haute Horology)      ('The Watch Guide', Studio)          (Messika & Rimowa)
```

#### **1. Backward Integration (Upstream Lineage & Brand Ownership)**
- **Parent Manufacturing Roots**: Ethos was founded and backed by **KDDL Limited** (holding 50.11%–50.12%), India’s leading manufacturer of precision watch components (dials and hands). This relationship gives Ethos technical horological heritage, deep Swiss watch industry networks, and technical credibility.
- **Equity Ownership in Watch Maisons & IP**: Ethos holds a **33.88%** stake in Swiss entity **Silvercity Brands AG** and owns **Favre Leuba GmbH**, reviving historical Swiss watch brands and capturing brand value directly from creation to retail. Silvercity debuted at *Watches and Wonders Geneva* in April 2026. Ethos also holds equity in independent Swiss watchmaker **Haute-rive Watches SA**.

#### **2. Forward Integration (Downstream Lifecycle & Direct-to-Consumer Capture)**
- **Omnichannel Retail & Digital Reach**: From physical boutiques to digital commerce, Ethos directly controls consumer touchpoints across 103 stores in 34 cities and native digital apps. It drives awareness and organic lead generation through its in-house content engine (*The Watch Guide*) and creative production house (*Ethos Studio*).
- **Certified Pre-Owned Ecosystem (*Second Movement*)**: Rather than losing customers when they upgrade or sell timepieces, Ethos authenticates, refurbishes, and resells pre-owned luxury watches, creating a circular trade-in engine.
- **After-Sales Service Infrastructure (*Ethos Watch Care* & *Micron*)**: By establishing service centres capable of repairing timepieces across 70+ luxury houses—bolstered by its specialized subsidiary **Micron Watch Services**—Ethos captures the recurring service lifecycle of the collector.
- **Adjacent Luxury Categories**: Broadening beyond horology, Ethos extends its luxury customer relationships into high jewellery (**Messika**) and high-end luggage (**RIMOWA**) through **Ethos Lifestyle Private Limited**.

# Growth Triggers

The principal growth triggers for Ethos Limited capable of driving significant top-line expansion and margin improvement include:

* **Expanding Exclusive Brand Agreements**: Ethos holds exclusive Indian retail rights for **64 brands** out of its 85+ brand portfolio. Exclusivity eliminates domestic retail price competition, protects pricing power, and commands higher gross margins compared to non-exclusive distribution.
* **Portfolio Premiumisation and Rising ASP**: The company is deliberately shifting its sales mix towards the **Luxury and High-Luxury segments**, which account for **71%** of watch sales. Consequently, the Average Selling Price (ASP) has climbed from ₹1.49 lakh in FY22 to **₹2.07 lakh in FY26** and **₹2.26 lakh in Q1 FY27**, driving strong revenue productivity per transaction and operating leverage.
* **Duty Reductions via the India–EFTA Trade Agreement**: The phased implementation of the India–EFTA Trade and Economic Partnership Agreement is expected to progressively moderate high basic customs duties on Swiss watches. This directly lowers procurement costs, facilitates new brand introductions, and provides substantial runway for gross margin improvement.
* **Operating Leverage from Store Maturation**: Ethos accelerated store openings from 73 boutiques in FY25 to **103 boutiques across 34 cities** by Q1 FY27. Because newer stores in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities are currently in their nascent phase with front-loaded rental and manpower costs, their natural maturation over 12–24 months will expand sales density and operating margins.
* **Scaling the Certified Pre-Owned (CPO) Market**: Branded under *Second Movement*, the pre-owned vertical operates in an underpenetrated space with limited organized competition and attractive unit economics. It captures younger aspirational buyers and generates high-margin circular trade-ins without heavy new inventory commitments.
* **Monetizing HNI Footfall via Luxury Lifestyle Verticals**: Leveraging its existing HNI customer base, Ethos is scaling non-watch luxury categories—such as **Messika** fine jewellery and **RIMOWA** luxury travel goods—under *Ethos Lifestyle Private Limited*. This broadens its total addressable market and extracts higher lifetime customer value with minimal incremental client acquisition costs.
