# Business Model

### **Overview of KDDL Limited’s Business Model**

1. **KDDL Limited** (formerly Kamla Dials and Devices Limited, established in 1981) is an Indian multinational company operating at the intersection of precision engineering and the global luxury watch ecosystem.
2. The company runs a dual-engine business model comprising **high-precision contract manufacturing** and **organized luxury retail and brand ownership**.
3. In its core manufacturing operations, KDDL is an export-driven original equipment manufacturer (OEM) producing critical watch components, including watch dials, hands, indexes, appliqués, and metal bracelets.
4. It supplies these micro-engineered components to the world’s leading Swiss and global luxury horology brands.
5. Operating across state-of-the-art facilities in India and Switzerland, the company leverages India’s cost-efficient, high-skill engineering base alongside Swiss precision heritage.
6. Beyond watch parts, KDDL leverages its micro-precision tooling and stamping capabilities through its **Eigen** division to produce high-tolerance stamped components and progressive tools.
7. The Eigen division caters to high-growth, mission-critical global industries, including automotive, aerospace, electricals, electronics, consumer durables, and clean energy/EVs.
8. To complement its luxury offerings, the company operates an ornamental packaging division (**Ornapac**) that designs and manufactures premium presentation boxes for watches, jewelry, and luxury lifestyle products.
9. On the consumer-facing front, KDDL is forward-integrated through its listed subsidiary, **Ethos Limited**.
10. Ethos is India’s largest organized luxury watch retail network, operating more than 100 experiential boutiques across major Indian cities.
11. Ethos sells premier luxury and high-luxury international watch brands, operates certified pre-owned (CPO) luxury watch sales, and provides specialized after-sales servicing.
12. KDDL also participates in luxury brand ownership through **Silvercity Brands AG**, which owns and is globally rolling out the historic Swiss heritage brand **Favre-Leuba**.
13. By combining backward-integrated precision component manufacturing with forward-integrated luxury retail and brand management, KDDL captures multiple margin pools across the luxury lifestyle value chain.

### **5. The Vertical Integration Story: Backward & Forward Integration**

KDDL is an example of an **end-to-end vertically integrated luxury horology and precision engineering enterprise**:

```
[ BACKWARD INTEGRATION ]           [ CORE MANUFACTURING ]            [ FORWARD INTEGRATION ]
In-House Tooling & Dies     ──►   Dials, Hands, Indexes    ──►    Ethos Luxury Retail Network
In-House Plating & Finishing ──►   Steel Watch Bracelets    ──►    Ornapac Luxury Packaging
Swiss Precision Hubs (Estima)──►  Precision Stamping (Eigen)──►  Favre-Leuba Heritage Watch Brand
```

#### **How the Company is Backward Integrated**
* **In-House Tool & Die Development:** Through Eigen and its tool rooms, KDDL designs and manufactures its own high-precision progressive stamping tools, dies, and molds, eliminating dependence on external toolmakers and securing intellectual property.
* **In-House Electroplating & Finishing:** The company is backward integrating its plating lines—bringing previously outsourced electroplating processes in-house at Eigen to control precision surface treatments, quality tolerances, and turnaround times.
* **Specialized Component Synergies:** The hands, indexes, and appliqués units feed directly into dial manufacturing, allowing KDDL to deliver complete, ready-to-fit dial assemblies with integrated artisanal finishes (stone cutting, enameling).
* **Swiss Craftsmanship Hub:** By acquiring **Estima AG** and **Pylania SA** in Switzerland, KDDL secured upstream Swiss watch hand and dial manufacturing heritage, allowing seamless technical collaboration with Swiss watch houses.

#### **How the Company is Forward Integrated**
* **Direct-to-Consumer Luxury Retail (Ethos Limited):** Instead of merely supplying components to watchmakers, KDDL forward-integrated into consumer retail via Ethos. With over 100 luxury boutiques, Ethos captures the retail distributor margin, provides authorized after-sales repair infrastructure, and manages the Certified Pre-Owned (CPO) luxury watch market in India.
* **Luxury Ornamental Packaging (Ornapac):** KDDL provides luxury packaging solutions to the same watch and jewelry clients purchasing its components, delivering complete design-to-display value.
* **Full Brand Ownership (Favre-Leuba):** KDDL completed its forward journey by acquiring and relaunching the historic Swiss watch brand **Favre-Leuba** through Silvercity Brands AG. This establishes KDDL as a full watchmaker, controlling design, manufacturing, brand marketing, and international distribution.

### **1. Value Chain of KDDL, Ornapac, and Ethos**

* **Upstream Manufacturing (KDDL):** KDDL manufactures high-precision exterior watch components, including **dials, hands, indexes, appliqués, and stainless steel bracelets** through micro-machining, stamping, and electroplating. These parts are supplied directly to international watch brands and Swiss horology maisons.
* **Complementary Packaging (Ornapac):** The packaging division designs and produces **luxury presentation boxes** using materials like wood, leather, metal, and sustainable paperboard. It supplies these finished presentation boxes to watch, jewelry, and lifestyle brands to house their timepieces and accessories.
* **Downstream Retail & Service (Ethos):** Ethos operates India's largest luxury retail boutique network (100+ stores). It procures finished luxury watches from international brand owners and distributes them to end consumers via retail boutiques, while providing **certified pre-owned (CPO) watch sales and after-sales servicing**.

### **2. Eigen Precision Engineering, High-Tolerance Stamping, and Target Sectors**

* **About Eigen Precision Engineering:** Established in 2006 in Bengaluru, Eigen is KDDL’s precision engineering division holding **IATF 16949, ISO 9001, and AS 9100D certifications**. In FY 2025–26, Eigen generated **~₹200 Crores in revenue** (39.4% of KDDL's turnover), growing **35.6% year-on-year**, driven by a **49.7% increase in export sales**.
* **High-Tolerance Stamping Tools:** Metal stamping involves shaping and cutting sheet metal into intricate parts using specialized press tools. High-tolerance stamping operates at **micron-level precision** using progressive tool/die designs that integrate multiple cutting, forming, and punching operations in high-speed, zero-defect production. Eigen engineers and produces these **progressive dies, tools, and close-tolerance metal parts** in-house, manufacturing components priced from **₹0.02 to ₹200 per piece**.
* **Sector Opportunities (EV, Alternate Energy, Aerospace, Automotive, Electricals):**
  * **Industry Tailwinds:** The global precision stamping market is expanding due to component miniaturization, vehicle lightweighting, and the "China Plus One" supply-chain realignment.
  * **Product Applications:** In electric vehicles and alternate energy, Eigen supplies parts for **energy storage solutions and electrical busbar products** to global OEMs.
  * **Operating Strategy:** Management positions Eigen as a **capability-selling partner** across sectors rather than a single-product seller, allowing it to adapt tooling to changing OEM component designs across automotive, aerospace, and electrical applications.
  * **Expansion:** To capture this export demand, KDDL is adding an additional **~30,000 sq. ft. facility in Bengaluru** in FY 2026–27 and executing a **backward integration capex to bring electroplating in-house** for improved quality control and volume scale.

**Stage 1: Manufacturing the tools (dies)**

Suppose an automotive OEM needs a tiny, highly precise metal component for an EV.

- Eigen designs and manufactures a specialised stamping tool (called a die).
- This die is fitted into a high-speed industrial press.
- A sheet of metal passes through the press.
- The die cuts, punches and bends the metal into the required shape.

**Stage 2: Manufacturing the components**

Eigen can also use these tools and presses to manufacture the actual components in large volumes and supply them to OEMs.

So, Eigen is not merely a tool manufacturer supplying precision engineering companies. It is also a contract manufacturer supplying precision-engineered metal parts directly to OEMs.

The ₹0.02–₹200 price range refers to the manufactured components, not necessarily the tools.

A busbar is essentially a thick strip or bar of copper or aluminium that carries and distributes electrical power.

Think of it as an electrical highway.

In an EV:

1. The battery pack contains multiple battery cells.
2. These cells need to be electrically connected.
3. Busbars connect battery cells and modules and help distribute high electrical currents.
4. They also connect major electrical components in the power distribution system.

Why is it important?

- Handles high electrical currents.
- Provides low-resistance electrical connections.
- Reduces wiring complexity.
- Helps make electrical connections compact and reliable.

Eigen manufactures precision metal components used in these electrical applications.

### **2. Durable Competitive Advantages and Defense Against Global Competitors**
* **Micron-Level Tooling Capabilities:** KDDL operates in-house design and fabrication of complex progressive stamping tools and dies, enabling high-speed, zero-defect production of intricate parts.
* **Decades-Long Horology Relationships:** The company holds 30+ years of entrenched supplier relationships with major Swiss luxury watch groups that require rigorous certification and audit processes.
* **Dual-Country Manufacturing Arbitrage:** KDDL combines cost-effective engineering and tooling talent in India with localized Swiss manufacturing and technical hubs (Estima AG and Pylania SA).
* **Competing on Value Over Price:** Management explicitly avoids competing on low-cost commodity parts against high-volume Asian manufacturers (e.g., China), focusing instead on high-value, high-complexity components.

# Growth Triggers

### **Key Growth Triggers for KDDL Limited**

---

### **1. Watch & Luxury Retail — Ethos Limited (~75% of Consolidated Revenue)**

* **Boutique Network Doubling (Sales Trigger):**
  Ethos crossed 100 boutiques across 34 cities in FY26 and plans to **double its store network to ~200 boutiques within the next 3 to 4 years**. This aggressive physical expansion targets both flagship locations in metros and under-penetrated Tier-2/Tier-3 cities.

* **TEPA Free Trade Agreement Implementation (Sales & Demand Trigger):**
  The India–EFTA Trade and Economic Partnership Agreement (TEPA), effective October 2025, lowered Indian basic customs duties on Swiss watches to 15.71% in January 2026, with a schedule to **phase duties down to zero by 2031**. This delivers domestic price parity with international markets, discouraging grey market/overseas purchases and accelerating Swiss export inflows into India.

* **Stabilization of Swiss Franc (CHF) & Price Pass-Through (Margin Trigger):**
  In FY26, the sharp appreciation of the Swiss Franc (~23% rise against the INR) elevated inventory purchase costs and squeezed gross margins. Because retail prices are adjusted semi-annually, **management guided that a 7% to 8% price increase remains to be fully passed on to consumers**. Once passed on, retail operating margins are expected to expand significantly.

* **Expansion into Ultra-Luxury and High-Margin Verticals (Margin & Sales Trigger):**
  Ethos is scaling up its **Certified Pre-Owned (CPO) luxury watch division** (capturing high-margin secondary trading), expanding the **ultra-luxury segment** (watches priced from ₹50 lakh to ₹1 crore+), and scaling lifestyle adjacencies (Messika high jewelry and Rimowa luxury travel luggage).

---

### **2. Precision & Watch Components (~23% of Consolidated Revenue)**

* **Eigen Division Expansion into Clean Energy & Aerospace (Sales Trigger):**
  Eigen grew by 35.6% YoY to ~₹200 Crores in FY26 (40.1% 5-year CAGR), driven by a 49.7% jump in exports. An additional **~30,000 sq. ft. precision stamping facility in Bengaluru is becoming operational in FY27** to fulfill orders for EV battery busbars, energy storage components, aerospace, and electronics OEMs.

* **In-House Plating Backward Integration (Margin Trigger):**
  KDDL is executing a backward integration project to bring **electroplating in-house for Eigen**. Eliminating outsourced finishing reduces vendor margins, improves process turnaround times, and secures higher blended operating profitability on close-tolerance metal parts.

* **Capacity Expansion in Stainless Steel Bracelets (Sales & Operating Leverage Trigger):**
  The bracelet division (commissioned in late 2024 at ~75,000 annual capacity with 75%–80% utilization in FY26) is **expanding capacity to 110,000–120,000 units per annum over the next 12 months**. Bracelets are exempt from the Swissness origin criteria, allowing KDDL to capture an estimated **₹50–75 Crore mid-to-long term opportunity** from global luxury brands.

* **Shift Toward Artisanal, High-Value Watch Components (Margin Trigger):**
  In the traditional dials and hands division, KDDL is shifting its production mix away from standard parts toward **high-margin artisanal techniques**—including semi-precious stone dials, enamel finishes, and gold hands—which carry significantly higher unit realizations.

* **Global Expansion Beyond Switzerland (Sales Trigger):**
  To hedge against cyclical European slowdowns, KDDL is expanding component exports into reviving horology and engineering markets across **the United States, Japan, Germany, and the United Kingdom**.
