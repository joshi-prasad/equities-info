### SECTION 3 — CONSOLIDATED SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

The performance of the three consolidated operating segments in Q1 FY27 is analyzed below [286]:

| Segment | Q1 FY27 Revenue | YoY | QoQ | Q1 FY27 Segment Result* | YoY |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **B2B** | 1,488.4 cr [286] | +17% [286] | -4% [286] | 114.6 cr [286] | -18% [286] |
| **B2C / Fluid Dynamics** | 301.0 cr [286] | +3% [286] | -11% [286] | 25.8 cr [286] | -7% [286] |
| **Financial Services** | 210.2 cr [286] | +9% [286] | -5% [286] | 9.4 cr [286] | -31% [286] |

*\*Segment results are defined as profit/(loss) before exceptional items, tax, and interest (and after interest for the Financial Services segment) [285].*

#### Key Segment Observations:
1. **The B2B segment was the primary driver of consolidated revenue growth**, expanding +17% YoY (an absolute increase of 212.1 cr), which offset softer performance in other segments [286].
2. **Profitability deteriorated across all three segments YoY**, with the Financial Services segment recording the sharpest earnings decline at -31% YoY, followed by B2B at -18% YoY and B2C at -7% YoY [286].
3. **Double-digit segment revenue growth did not translate into segment profit growth**, as B2B segment results declined by -18% YoY despite a +17% revenue increase due to higher raw material inputs and export logistics headwinds [286, 295].

### SECTION 4 — WHAT DROVE THE CONSOLIDATED RESULT?

#### Positive Drivers
| Driver | Evidence | Short Interpretation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Robust Domestic Demand** | Standalone domestic sales grew +16% YoY, led by Powergen (+18%), Industrial (+19%), and Distribution & Aftermarket (+20%) [273, 290]. | Robust domestic demand across all segments offset weak export markets and supported the topline [289]. |
| **Institutional Industrial Growth** | Marine segment revenue grew +125% YoY (more than doubled) and Railways grew +62% YoY in Q1 FY27 [291]. | Strong execution on durable platform relationships supported industrial segment momentum [291]. |
| **Landmark Hyperscale Data Center Win** | Secured first major hyperscale data center order of approximately 192 MW in Q1 FY27 [302]. | breakthrough contract win establishes KOEL as a credible alternate for ultra-high horsepower hyperscalers [302]. |
| **Landmark Natural Gas Order** | Received order for natural gas gensets up to 500 kVA in the oil & gas industrial segment [292, 323]. | The order reinforces KOEL's capability in CPCB IV-compliant gas solutions and fuel-agnostic technology [292, 322]. |

#### Negative Drivers
| Driver | Evidence | Short Interpretation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Geopolitical Export Slowdown** | Standalone B2B exports fell -11% YoY (106 cr vs. 120 cr) and international markets experienced significant delays [273, 293]. | Delayed customer investment decisions and West Asia conflict logjams negatively impacted segment mix and leverage [293]. |
| **West Asia Logistical Blockage** | Conflict in West Asia choked logistics corridors, delaying delivery of secured order backlogs [308, 309]. | Supply chain bottlenecks directly restricted fulfillment and revenue recognition of exports in Q1 FY27 [308, 309]. |
| **Commodity Cost Inflation** | Massive increases in global commodity prices contracted margins in Q1 FY27 [325]. | High input material inflation compressed gross profitability margins across operating segments [325]. |
| **Employee and Capability Investments** | Consolidated employee expenses rose +39% YoY (+54.1 cr) to 191.2 cr in Q1 FY27 [284]. | High personnel costs from annual increments, ESOPs, and strategic hires temporarily weighed on operating margins [304]. |
