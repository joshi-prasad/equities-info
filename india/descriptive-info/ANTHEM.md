# Business Model

**Anthem Biosciences Limited** is an innovation-driven, technology-focused **Contract Research, Development, and Manufacturing Organization (CRDMO)** headquartered in India.

The company operates an **integrated, end-to-end business model** supporting global pharmaceutical and emerging biotechnology innovators across the entire drug lifecycle—from early-stage target identification and drug discovery to preclinical research, clinical trial material supply, and commercial-scale GMP manufacturing. Anthem is differentiated by having comprehensive in-house capabilities across both **small molecules** (New Chemical Entities - NCE) and **large molecules** (New Biological Entities - NBE). Its technology platforms encompass advanced therapeutic modalities, including peptides, oligonucleotides, antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs), RNA-based technologies, high-potency APIs (HPAPIs), biocatalysis, and specialized fermentation.

---

### **Business Verticals**
Anthem operates through **two primary reportable business segments**:

1. **CRDMO Services**:
   * **Scope**: Offers custom research, process development, analytical testing, and commercial manufacturing solutions across small molecules and biologics.
   * **Revenue Contribution**: Represents the vast majority of the company's business (~80%–85% of total revenues). Revenue within CRDMO is driven by commercial manufacturing (~65%), with the remainder split between R&D services and clinical development supplies.

2. **Specialty Ingredients**:
   * **Scope**: A product-focused business segment dedicated to manufacturing and selling specialized fermentation-based active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), probiotics, enzymes, nutritional actives, vitamin analogues, and biosimilars catering to health and nutrition industries.
   * **Revenue Contribution**: Serves as a steady revenue backstop (~15%–20% of total revenues), primarily domestic-focused in India.

### **Commercial & Research Molecule Pipeline**

Anthem Biosciences tracks its CRDMO drug pipeline across three distinct stages:

* **Commercial Molecules**: As of **Q2 FY27**, Anthem has **15 molecules under commercial development/production** (up from 14 in FY26/Q1 FY27, after 1 late-stage molecule received regulatory approval in Q2 FY27). Commercial manufacturing contributes **~65%** of CRDMO segment revenue.
* **Late-Stage (Phase III) Molecules**: **9 molecules** are currently in late-stage Phase III clinical development (down from 10 following the recent commercial launch). Management expects several of these to transition to commercial scale over the next 18–24 months upon trial completion.
* **Early-Stage Research & Development / RFQs**: Anthem is active across **100+ early-stage programmes** (spanning target identification, lead optimization, and pre-clinical/early clinical stages). Over 100 of these active projects are partnered with emerging biotech innovators.
* **Client Breadth**: The pipeline is supported across **9 Large Pharma**, **20 Mid Pharma**, and **100+ Emerging Biotech / Small Pharma clients**, with over 8,000 global projects handled historically.

### **Phase III Pipeline & Commercial Transition**

#### **1. Commercial Conversion Rate from Phase III**
* **Conversion Track Record**: Management expects Phase III molecules to transition as trials complete, though not all succeed due to clinical trial risks. In **Q2 FY27**, 1 late-stage molecule received regulatory approval and transitioned to commercial production, bringing total commercial molecules to **15** and reducing late-stage molecules to **9**. Over the prior 18 months, **5 molecules** successfully moved into commercial production.
* **Industry Conversion Rate**: Industry benchmark data indicates a **~83% probability of success** for drugs progressing through Phase III and regulatory approval.

---

#### **2. Average Revenue per Commercial Molecule**
* **Average Contribution**: Management and analyst consensus indicate that an average commercial molecule yields roughly **\$12 million (~₹100 Crore) annually** in CRDMO revenue.
* **Variability Drivers**: Actual revenue varies widely across individual molecules based on:
  1. **Supply Scope**: Anthem commands higher revenues when supplying the full **Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient (API)** versus **advanced intermediates**. The 5 recently commercialized molecules represent an equal mix of APIs and advanced intermediates.
  2. **Indication & Dosages**: High-dosage therapeutics or major broad-market diseases generate significantly higher revenue than initial niche/orphan indications.
  3. **End-Market Scale**: The current commercial portfolio addresses end-markets with combined peak end-product sales exceeding **\$10 billion**.

---

#### **3. Transition Timeline (Phase III to Commercial)**
* **Timeline**: The transition from Phase III clinical trials to full commercial supply typically takes **18 to 24 months**.
* **Variability**: Some molecules move faster if regulatory reviews are expedited, while others experience shifts depending on trial readout dates or client launch deferrals across global markets.

---

#### **4. Commercial Revenue Concentration**
* **Segment Share**: Commercial manufacturing represents **~65.3% of total company revenue** (~85% of CRDMO segment revenue).
* **Customer Concentration**:
  * **Top 5 Customers**: Generate **~50%** of total company revenue.
  * **Top 10 Customers**: Generate **~80%** of total company revenue.

# Growth Triggers

During the **Q2 FY27 earnings conference call**, management highlighted several key structural and operational growth triggers that support the company's long-term revenue momentum and profitability:

### **1. Commercialization of Late-Stage Pipeline**
* **Molecule Transition**: In Q2 FY27, 1 late-stage Phase III molecule received regulatory approval and transitioned into commercial production, bringing Anthem's active commercial portfolio to **15 molecules** (and leaving 9 in late-stage Phase III).
* **Significant Market Potential**: Over the prior 18 months, 5 molecules transitioned to commercial scale. Management noted that the estimated peak end-market potential for these newly commercialized assets exceeds **\$10 billion**.
* **Headroom in Existing Commercial Portfolio**: Existing commercial assets continue to grow, with patent expiries for key molecules far off (ranging from 2032 to nearly 2040).

---

### **2. Big Pharma M&A & Account Expansion**
* **Biotech Acquisitions as a Growth Multiplier**: A significant portion of Anthem's early-stage development projects with emerging biotechs are being acquired by Big Pharma companies. Once acquired, Big Pharma deploys substantial commercial budgets to scale up global launches, creating large-scale supply demand for Anthem.
* **Direct Big Pharma Onboarding**: Anthem now works with **8–9 Big Pharma clients**. Beyond M&A, Big Pharma companies are approaching Anthem directly for new projects and supply chain diversification.

---

### **3. Revival in Global Biotech Funding & Higher RFP/RFQ Inflows**
* **Increased Inquiries**: Management observed a clear improvement in Request for Proposal (RFP) and Request for Quotation (RFQ) inflows in recent months, driven by a gradual recovery in global biotech funding.
* **Deep Early-Stage Pipeline**: Over 100 early-stage programs with emerging biotechs provide a broad pipeline of potential future commercial molecules.

---

### **4. Scaling Advanced Modalities ("New Tech")**
* **Differentiated Capabilities**: Anthem has invested in advanced modalities for over a decade, transitioning projects from lab-scale to commercial scale across **oligonucleotides, peptides, antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs), RNAi, and high-potency APIs (HPAPIs)**.
* **Peptide Expansion**: Peptide applications are expanding beyond GLP-1s into large therapeutic areas like oncology and immunology.
* **Forward Integration into Drug Products**: Anthem is expanding from drug substance manufacturing into **biologics drug product (fill-finish) facilities** to offer complete end-to-end solutions.

---

### **5. Strategic Capacity Expansion & Unit IV Infrastructure**
* **Ramp-Up at Unit III (NeoAnthem)**: Capacity utilization at Unit III is increasing due to new projects in peptides, oncology, and pilot scale-ups.
* **Unit IV Greenfield Construction**: Construction is underway on the 30-acre **Unit IV facility** (₹1,200+ Cr Phase I investment adding 365 KL custom synthesis and 100 KL fermentation capacity), scheduled for commissioning by March 2028 (end of FY28) to support commercial-scale demand.

---

### **6. Supply Chain Self-Sufficiency & Backward Integration**
* **In-House Manufacturing**: Reduced reliance on Chinese sourcing and increased domestic backward integration for key intermediates and biocatalysts have improved raw material security and yield.
* **Proprietary Technology Platforms**: Adoption of flow chemistry, biocatalysis, and continuous processing reduces cost of goods, cementing industry-leading EBITDA margins (50.6% in Q2 FY27).

# Management Guidences
Beyond top-line revenue and profitability margins, the management team provided key operational and financial guidance across several areas during the **Q1 FY27** and **Q2 FY27** earnings calls and investor presentations:

### **1. Capital Expenditure (Capex) & Expansion Timeline**
* **Unit IV Investment**: Management reaffirmed its Capex guidance of **₹1,200 Crores** for Phase I of the 30-acre greenfield Unit IV facility.

---

### **2. Effective Tax Rate & Subsidiary Profitability**
* **Tax Normalization**: Management expects the effective tax rate to normalize to **25%–25.5% for FY27**.
* **NeoAnthem (Unit III) Turnaround**: The higher tax rate in FY26 was partly due to unabsorbed losses at the Unit III subsidiary (NeoAnthem). With capacity utilization ramping up from 15% in FY26 to 30%–35% in Q1 FY27, management expects NeoAnthem to **break even and potentially turn profitable on a full-year basis in FY27**.

---

### **3. ESOP Expense Trajectory**

  * **FY26 Actual**: ~₹16 Crores

---

### **5. Asset Turnover & Capacity Ramping**

## 8. Synthesis & Key Observations

1. **Revenue Growth vs EBITDA Growth**: Over FY23–FY26, Revenue expanded at 26.22% CAGR while Reported EBITDA grew at 20.21% CAGR (Core Operating EBITDA at 24.65% CAGR). In FY26, core operating EBITDA grew **24.40%** YoY on a 15.17% revenue increase, reflecting strong operating leverage.
2. **Expanding Incremental Margins**: Core incremental EBITDA margins reached **58.54%** in FY26 (83.18% on reported EBITDA), driven by higher commercial batch volumes and shift toward high-margin APIs and advanced modalities.
3. **Operating Leverage Demonstration**: In high-revenue quarters like Q2 FY27 (₹594 Crore revenue), operating leverage expanded EBITDA margin to **50.6%** (45.7% core), illustrating the power of fixed-cost absorption.
4. **Cash Flow Conversion Acceleration**: Operating cash flow conversion surged to **101.09%** of Core EBITDA (126% on operating profit) in FY26, generating ₹844.00 Crore in CFO and ₹632.80 Crore in Free Cash Flow.
5. **Working Capital Efficiency**: Cash Conversion Cycle compressed from 202 days in FY25 to **124 days** in FY26 due to a 111-day reduction in inventory holding days.
6. **Return Profile Expansion**: ROCE improved consistently from 24% in FY24 to 28% in FY25 and **30% in FY26**, while ROE expanded to **21.71%**.
7. **J-Curve & Non-Linear Capital Verification**: Anthem's financial trajectory confirms classic **J-Curve characteristics**:
   - **Phase 1 (Capital Deployment & Gestation)**: Upfront Capex (e.g., Unit III buildout in FY24) creates temporary CWIP build-up and margin compression.
   - **Phase 2 (Commercial Scale & Inflection)**: As customer trial molecules transition to Phase III and commercial production (15 commercial molecules, 9 Phase III), utilization ramps from 15% to 75%, generating incremental EBITDA margins of 58%–83% and driving ROCE above 30%.
