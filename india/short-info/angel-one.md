# Angel One Ltd. – Investment Summary

## Company Snapshot

Angel One is evolving from a traditional discount broker into a technology-led financial platform that monetizes customer engagement across investing, leverage, wealth management and financial distribution.

Unlike traditional brokerage firms that primarily earn transaction fees, Angel One increasingly operates as a:

**Retail financial ecosystem that acquires customers through broking, monetizes engagement through Margin Trading Facility (MTF), and gradually expands customer lifetime value through wealth management, mutual funds, credit distribution, insurance and asset management.**

Its business currently operates through two distinct economic engines:

| Business | Economic Role |
|----------|---------------|
| Broking | Customer acquisition + transaction cash engine |
| MTF / Funding Business | Current profit engine + revenue diversification |

Management is investing aggressively in:

- Wealth Management (Ionic)
- Asset Management (AMC)
- Loan Against Securities (NBFC)
- Credit Distribution
- Insurance
- AI-driven platform capabilities

to gradually transform the business from:

**Broker → Platform → Financial Ecosystem**

---

# Core Investment Thesis

The investment thesis is **NOT** higher retail trading activity alone.

Nor is it simply about acquiring more brokerage clients.

The current opportunity is driven by:

- increasing revenue per active client,
- rapid expansion of the Margin Trading Facility (MTF),
- improving customer monetization,
- AI-driven cross-selling,
- operating leverage,
- and gradual diversification into recurring financial products.

Over the last several years Angel One invested heavily in:

- digital platform infrastructure,
- AI capabilities,
- customer acquisition,
- branding,
- wealth management,
- asset management,
- and financial distribution.

The business is now transitioning from:

**Customer Acquisition Phase → Customer Monetization Phase**

The key question is no longer:

**"Can Angel One acquire more brokerage accounts?"**

The key question is:

**"Can Angel One successfully monetize its large retail customer base by increasing engagement, funding penetration and recurring financial revenues while reducing dependence on brokerage income?"**

# Business Model Summary

## 1. Broking (Customer Acquisition Engine)

The historical foundation of the business.

Products include:

- Equity trading
- F&O
- Commodity trading
- Depository services

Advantages:

- Large retail franchise
- Strong digital platform
- Low onboarding friction
- Large Tier-2/Tier-3 presence

Strategic role today:

- acquires customers,
- generates transaction revenues,
- creates behavioral data,
- feeds the ecosystem.

Although brokerage remains nearly 60% of revenue, management is deliberately reducing dependence on this business.

---

## 2. Margin Trading Facility (Current Earnings Engine)

The most important business inside Angel One today.

The company provides funding to clients purchasing delivery shares.

This business generates:

- Interest income
- Higher customer lifetime value
- Better revenue density
- Superior operating leverage

The funding book has expanded rapidly and now represents the largest source of diversification away from brokerage.

Unlike wealth management, MTF is already contributing materially to earnings.

This is currently the strongest economic engine inside the platform.

---

## 3. Credit Distribution (Emerging Platform Business)

Angel One distributes:

- Personal loans
- Financial products

through partner banks and NBFCs.

Importantly:

- Angel One does not take underwriting risk.
- Lending capital remains on partner balance sheets.

The company monetizes:

- customer data,
- AI scoring,
- and customer relationships

through high distribution commissions.

This is an attractive asset-light business with significant scaling potential.

---

## 4. Wealth Management & Mutual Funds (Future Recurring Revenue)

Management is building:

- Ionic Wealth
- Mutual Fund Distribution
- Passive AMC

These businesses currently consume capital and depress margins.

However, if successful they can transform Angel One from:

transaction-based revenues

toward

recurring AUM-based revenues.

This remains one of the company's largest strategic priorities.

---

## 5. NBFC / Loan Against Securities (New Capital Allocation Layer)

The recent NBFC initiative marks an important strategic shift.

Historically Angel One preferred an asset-light distribution model.

Now management intends to deploy balance-sheet capital through:

- Loan Against Securities (LAS)

This potentially improves yields but also introduces:

- balance-sheet risk,
- credit exposure,
- and greater capital intensity.

# Why The Moat Exists

The moat is **not** discount brokerage.

The moat is:

**Large Retail Customer Base + Technology Platform + AI-driven Customer Intelligence + Multi-product Financial Ecosystem**

Key evidence:

- 37+ million retail clients
- Massive behavioral data
- AI-enabled servicing
- Strong digital onboarding
- Cross-selling capabilities
- Large MTF platform
- High engagement ecosystem

The strongest competitive advantage is:

**The ability to continuously increase customer lifetime value without proportionally increasing acquisition costs.**

Brokerage platforms can be replicated.

Customer engagement ecosystems are much harder to replicate.

# Bull / Base /Bear Framework

## Bull Case

- MTF book continues expanding
- Interest spreads remain healthy
- Wealth business reaches breakeven
- Distribution revenues scale materially
- AI improves customer monetization
- EBITDA margins return toward 40–45%
- Revenue mix becomes structurally diversified

---

## Base Case

- Broking remains dominant
- MTF continues growing
- Wealth gradually scales
- Credit distribution expands steadily
- Brokerage remains cyclical
- Operating leverage improves gradually

- Retail trading activity weakens
- SEBI introduces further regulatory tightening
- Borrowing costs increase
- MTF spreads compress
- Wealth incubation remains loss-making
- Customer acquisition costs remain elevated
- Platform diversification disappoints

# Final Investment View

Angel One appears to be transitioning from:

- a discount brokerage,
- transaction-driven business,
- market-cycle dependent earnings model

toward

- a digital financial ecosystem,
- customer monetization platform,
- engagement-driven earnings model.

The current earnings engine remains:

- Brokerage
- Margin Trading Facility (MTF)

while future value creation is expected from:

- Wealth Management
- Credit Distribution
- Mutual Funds
- Asset Management
- Insurance
- Loan Against Securities
- AI-driven customer monetization

Importantly, management's "financial ecosystem" narrative is only partially reflected in today's financial statements.

The majority of diversification has come from:

**Interest income generated through the expanding MTF book rather than fee-based financial products.**

The key question is no longer:

**"Can Angel One acquire more customers?"**

The key question is:

**"Can Angel One successfully transform millions of brokerage users into multi-product financial customers while maintaining healthy funding spreads and reducing dependence on cyclical trading activity?"**

If management succeeds, FY27-FY30 could see meaningful improvement in:

- Earnings quality
- Revenue diversification
- Customer lifetime value
- Operating leverage
- Capital efficiency
- Recurring revenues

The single most important variable to monitor is:

**Growth of the Client Funding Book (MTF) and its conversion into sustainable Interest Income while preserving funding spreads.**
